# ⚡ Quick Start: Get Found on Google in 3 Steps

## 🎯 Goal: Search "Xoli Nxiweni" and find your portfolio

---

## Step 1: Deploy Your Site (15 minutes)

### Easiest Method - Vercel:
1. Go to https://vercel.com
2. Sign up with GitHub
3. Click "New Project"
4. Import your portfolio repo
5. Click "Deploy"
6. **Copy your URL** (e.g., `your-portfolio.vercel.app`)

**✅ Done! Your site is now live.**

---

## Step 2: Submit to Google (10 minutes)

### Google Search Console:
1. Go to https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Enter your deployed URL
4. Choose verification method:
   - **Easiest**: HTML tag method
   - Copy the meta tag Google gives you
   - I'll help you add it to your HTML
5. Click "Verify"
6. Once verified:
   - Go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
   - Enter: `https://your-url.com/sitemap.xml`
   - Click "Submit"
7. Go to "URL Inspection"
   - Enter your homepage URL
   - Click "Request Indexing"

**✅ Done! Google will index your site in 24-48 hours.**

---

## Step 3: Build Presence (Ongoing)

### Update These Profiles (Add Portfolio Link):

1. **LinkedIn** (Most Important):
   - Go to your profile
   - Add portfolio URL to:
     - Featured section
     - About section
     - Experience descriptions

2. **GitHub**:
   - Update profile README
   - Add portfolio link to bio

3. **Share on Social Media**:
   - Post about your portfolio on LinkedIn
   - Share on Twitter/X
   - Share on Instagram story

**✅ Done! This helps Google find you faster.**
